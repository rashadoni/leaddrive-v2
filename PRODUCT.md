# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LeadDrive is used by tenant owners, CRM admins, sales/support managers, and operators who need to run customer conversations, sales workflows, routing, automation, reporting, and channel setup from one authenticated workspace. In omni-channel surfaces, the primary users are tenant admins configuring providers and inbox operators who need messages to arrive in a predictable, explainable way.

## Product Purpose

LeadDrive is a multi-tenant CRM and operations platform. The product should make complex business workflows feel controlled: customer data, channels, automations, AI agents, sales activity, and analytics must be visible, actionable, and safe for each tenant.

For omni-channel parity work, success means channel setup and inbox automation are as understandable as mature tools like respond.io while staying native to LeadDrive's own product language, tenant model, provider constraints, and Azerbaijani/Russian/English operator context.

## Brand Personality

Practical, precise, and operationally confident. LeadDrive should feel like a serious business system: direct labels, clear next steps, restrained visual treatment, and enough guidance that an admin can finish setup without guessing what a field or button does.

## Anti-references

- Unexplained configuration screens where provider setup, credentials, routing, and testing are mixed into one list.
- Generic modals that ask users to choose a channel type again after they already selected a channel.
- Dark-mode-only borrowed layouts or color schemes that feel foreign to LeadDrive.
- Decorative UI that looks impressive but hides what the user must do next.
- Jargon-heavy setup copy that assumes the admin already understands Meta, Chatwoot, ATL, WABA, webhooks, and token requirements.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious: every setup screen should show what to prepare, what to enter, and how to verify success.
2. Separate provider work from LeadDrive work: users should know what happens in Meta, Chatwoot, ATL, email, or SMS systems versus what is saved in LeadDrive.
3. Preserve tenant safety: UI clarity must not bypass auth, permissions, tenant scoping, provider constraints, or external-send safeguards.
4. Prefer familiar product patterns: cards, forms, tabs, dialogs, and navigation should behave consistently across settings, inbox, and automation surfaces.
5. Localize for real operators: Russian, Azerbaijani, and English copy should be direct, specific, and actionable, not literal placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use product-grade accessibility as the baseline: keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast for body and muted text, clear error copy, no information conveyed only by color, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and responsive layouts that do not create horizontal overflow on narrow screens.
