## 1. Azerbaijan

### 1a. Working time

| Topic | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Normal time (tam iş vaxtı) | Daily norm at most 8 h; weekly norm at most 40 h. | Art. 89, LC5 |
| 5-day / 6-day week | 5-day week with two rest days is the default; a 6-day week may be set. Under a 6-day week the day is at most 7 h (40-h norm), 6 h (36-h norm), 4 h (24-h norm). | Art. 90, LC5 |
| Shortened time (qısaldılmış iş vaxtı) | At most 24 h/week under age 16. At most 36 h/week for ages 16–18, persons with disability (61–100 % impairment), pregnant women, women with a child under 1.5, single parents of a child under 3. | Art. 91(2), LC5 |
| Shortened time, conditions | At most 36 h/week for harmful conditions (Art. 92) and for special-character work such as doctors and teachers (Art. 93). Read as a summary, not verbatim. | LC5 |
| Part-time (natamam iş vaxtı) | By agreement, as a shorter day or shorter week. The employer must grant it on request for pregnancy, disability, a chronically ill child or family member, or a woman with a child under 14 or a child with disability. Pay is proportional. | Art. 94, 245(1), LC5, LC10 |

- **Transfer:** a rest day coinciding with a non-working holiday moves to the working day right after the holiday (Art. 105(5), LC6).
- **Gurban/Ramadan:** if they coincide with another non-working holiday, the next working day is a rest day (Art. 105(6), LC6).
- **Bridging:** the executive authority may swap working and rest days to keep them consecutive (Art. 105(7), LC6).
