Help for HRRead attendance and handle requests

Setup and access

Attendance is off by default. An administrator enables it in MTM company settings. Managers see employees within their scope; access to view all employees is granted separately. Permission to make decisions is checked separately from viewing.

Day status and unknown hours

Employees start and finish their own day. Hours remain provisional while it is open. If a finish is missing or records conflict, hours are unknown, not zero. A manager does not close the day on behalf of an employee.

Requests and corrections

Employees submit requests for leave, absence or time corrections. A reviewer approves or rejects a request with a reason. A correction changes attendance only after approval; original marks are retained. Both dates are entered for an overnight shift.

Shifts and overtime

Published schedules define working days and hours. In the monthly timesheet, an overnight shift belongs to its start or end date, as chosen in the schedule. Actual work above plan and approved hours are shown separately. This is not a payroll calculation.

Leave balances

Entitlements and opening balances are entered from confirmed organization records. A reliable balance is not calculated when required information is missing. Approved future leave is shown separately from days already used.

Closed month

Check the timesheet before saving the month snapshot. A closed snapshot and its Excel/PDF files do not change. Later time corrections go through an employee request and appear in the journal. The reason is available in that request under its usual access rules.

Excel, PDF and email

Exports match the selected report layout, period and employees you can access. A closed month exports its saved snapshot. Scheduled email has no default recipients and is disabled initially. Add addresses and enable it when needed.

Location and privacy

Location collection stays off until approved consent texts are available. Time tracking continues to work. Biometrics are not used. Location during breaks is neither recorded nor included in reports. A risk flag calls for a review; it is not proof of a violation.
