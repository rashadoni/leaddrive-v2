Reading the numbers

Starts and finishes count received marks with location information or its absence. An employee may appear in several branches; the total counts each employee once.

Outside and uncertain reflect the saved location assessment and do not confirm presence at a branch.

When information is missing

A dash means no location assessment exists. It is neither zero nor an outside mark. Older days are excluded from these numeric totals and are not reassessed against today’s settings.

Break locations are not included in the report.
